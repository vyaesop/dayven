Privacy Policy

Dayven — Effective date: June 3, 2026

Dayven ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal productivity application. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Data you provide

  • Events and calendars — titles, dates, times, locations, notes, attendees, and reminders you enter.
  • Account information — email address used to create your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Preferences — theme, layout settings, and notification preferences stored on your device.

2. Data collected automatically

  • Crash reports — anonymized crash logs collected via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging token — a device identifier used to deliver push notifications. Not shared with third parties.

3. How we use your data

  • To store and sync your events across devices (cloud sync mode only).
  • To send you event reminders you have configured.
  • To diagnose and fix application crashes.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties.

4. Data storage

If you use On This Device mode, all data is stored locally in SQLite on your phone and never leaves your device.

If you use Sync Across Devices mode, your events and calendars are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon (neon.tech) in the United States, and your account is managed by Google Firebase Authentication.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication, push messaging, and crash reporting.
  • Neon — cloud database hosting (cloud sync mode only).

6. Account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Menu → My Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your events, calendars, and account credentials from our servers. Local data on your device is also cleared.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated via the app's "What's New" section.
